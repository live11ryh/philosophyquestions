## Style Prompt

Create an elegant editorial exhibition page for a Chinese philosophy and physics essay. The page should feel like a museum wall label expanded into a scholarly longform article: warm paper, deep ink, restrained bronze and oxblood accents, generous whitespace, crisp hierarchy, and portraits treated as archival objects rather than decoration.

## Colors

- Paper: `#f6efe3`
- Ink: `#171513`
- Muted ink: `#62584d`
- Bronze: `#a5743f`
- Oxblood: `#7d2f2a`
- Blue-black: `#1f3642`

## Typography

- Chinese body: `"Noto Serif SC"`, `"Songti SC"`, `"SimSun"`, serif
- Interface and labels: `"Inter"`, `"Segoe UI"`, sans-serif

## What NOT to Do

- Do not use generic blue dashboard styling.
- Do not use neon gradients, floating blobs, or glossy cards.
- Do not crowd the long essay; make section rhythm easy to scan.
- Do not let portraits overpower the text.
- Do not use oversized marketing-style hero copy unrelated to the document.
